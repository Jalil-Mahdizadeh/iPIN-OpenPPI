# DEC-0011: Accept Blueprint Amendment 001 and authorize negative-evidence audit

**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Status:** Accepted and effective
**Decision owner:** iPIN-OpenPPI expert group
**Execution owner:** Codex

## Decision

The expert group explicitly accepts Blueprint Amendment 001 and adds the
`negative_evidence_discovery_audit_v1` work package specified in the accepted
amendment.

This decision supersedes the approval state, but not the immutable evidentiary
reasoning, of `DEC-0010`. Resolution Path 3 of ISSUE-0003 is now the binding
primary design: reference-sequence PU ranking with a non-probabilistic,
symmetric compatibility/prioritization score.

## Human approval evidence

The expert-group instruction received on 2026-08-04 was explicit: “Accept
Blueprint Amendment 001 with an additional negative-evidence discovery work
package,” followed by the required audit scope and the binding statement that
no negative record may be interpreted as a universal nonbinding pair without
explicit supporting evidence.

This is the explicit approval event required by Section 20 of the proposal. It
is not inferred from silence or absence of comments.

## Immediate authorization

Codex is authorized to:

- finalize the accepted amendment and machine-readable policy;
- audit terms and acquire the four complete Negatome 2.0 protein-pair files;
- parse, map, reconcile, tier, validate, and report the specified negative
  evidence;
- inspect the existing frozen IntAct negative records and permitted current
  direct-positive sources;
- survey other public attempted/evaluable non-detection screens; and
- assess bounded PNU feasibility.

All scientific work must run through the pinned Arrhenius Apptainer image.

## Interpretation ceiling

Every negative or non-detection remains conditional on the evidence actually
available for its assay, constructs, orientation, species, publication, and
conditions. PDB non-contact is a different evidence family from experimental
non-detection. Missing context is not inferred. Positive conflicts are retained.

No universal nonbinding class is authorized.

## Non-authorizations

This decision does not authorize candidate-pair materialization, label or
positive/unlabeled indicator construction, pseudo-negatives, splits, structural
training labels, models, training, selection, release, or any experimental
validation claim. The audit must return to governance with independently
validated findings before another construction gate can advance.
