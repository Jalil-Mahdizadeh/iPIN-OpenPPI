# DEC-0049: Explain the direct-similarity signal before more modelling

Date: 2026-09-11. Bounded authorization from the user's request to take the
best next research step, then commit and push.

The external BioPlex challenge did not establish incremental learned-model
value over direct sequence similarity. Authorize an explanatory diagnostic on
that **already examined** evidence: order-free composition, composition-preserving
sequence shuffles, and within-bait comparisons matched on partner composition,
length and, in a second tier, the two direct-similarity scores. Freeze all rules
before computing these new diagnostics. Do not present this as a new external
validation, an externally registered study, or a rescue of DEC-0048.

Use only the existing public-training sequence metadata and arrays, the six
frozen DEC-0048 panels/score tables, and their component-bootstrap draws. No
new fitting, encoder extraction, source acquisition, development evaluation,
protected package/key access, quarantined-panel use or change to older artifacts.
The two cell lines remain related AP-MS co-association sources with missing
pair-level opportunity logs. Composition and similarity may encode real biology;
neither a shuffle nor matching proves confounding or direct binding.

Publish all prespecified outcomes, including insufficient matching support or
Monte Carlo instability. No caliper relaxation, replicate extension, selective
panel choice or outcome-driven model modification. The architecture/complexity
stop stays closed regardless of diagnostic results. Localization, complex
membership and a genuinely fresh direct-binary opportunity-aware test remain
separate future work, not claims addressed by this authorization.

Commit and push the implementation, aggregate evidence, concise report and
navigation/status updates. Keep raw data, endpoint-level derived matrices and
individual pair scores local, following existing publication boundaries.
